# PipTrail revenue streams (ranked by realism)
1. **Pro subscription** (manual Orange Money/MyZaka first, then Polar cards + Google Play). Free: 5 coach Qs/day, 2 partners, ads. Pro: unlimited, no ads.
2. **Groups/mentors**: paid dashboards for trading communities (discipline scores, shared lessons). Higher value per customer.
3. **Ads (Android, AdMob)**: built in for free users, banner on Journal/Stats/Crew only. Expect small income; payouts to Botswana come by cheque or wire, monthly.
4. **Broker referral (IB/affiliate)**: income from clients you refer. Disclose it clearly and check licensing; it conflicts with a discipline-first brand.
5. **Sponsorships/partnerships**: local trading educators, prop-style challenges. Needs an audience first.
6. **Expand regions**: same stack works across Southern/East Africa.
Never market returns or sell signals without legal advice.

## Mentor groups (built)
Mentors need Pro to create a group; students join free. Suggested pricing to test: mentor plan priced above individual Pro (e.g. 3-5x), because one mentor brings many students. Add a per-group student cap later if you want tiers.
